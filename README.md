# SQL-Analysis-for-Product-Quality-and-Pricing-Insights-at-FoodYum
In this project, I analyzed product data for FoodYum, a major U.S.-based grocery chain focused on offering affordable options across all food categories. 

As rising food costs impact customers, FoodYum wants to ensure a balanced product lineup that supports a wide range of budgets. Using SQL, I identified products missing year_added information due to a 2022 system bug, evaluated price ranges across different product types, and examined meat and dairy products with strong sales performance.

The insights support better inventory planning, pricing strategy, and product assortment decisions across the company’s stores.
